Welcome to North Star Bakery

A warm welcome

At North Star Bakery, the day begins before sunrise. We make fresh bread with simple ingredients, local grains, and enough time to let every loaf develop its flavor.

What we are known for

Our neighborhood bakery focuses on handmade breads, everyday pastries, and treats for celebrations. We welcome families, commuters, and anyone looking for something freshly baked.

Hours and location

We serve our local neighborhood. Please contact the bakery to confirm its current address, opening hours, and pickup availability before visiting.

Featured item of the week

Our featured selection is a basket of freshly baked breads and pastries, made for sharing or enjoying at home.
