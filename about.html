Our Story

A neighborhood bakery with a simple idea

North Star Bakery believes good bread should never be rushed. Our mornings begin early so each batch has the attention and time it deserves. We hope every visit feels welcoming and familiar.

How we choose ingredients

We value simple recipes, local grains, and thoughtful preparation. Our approach is to focus on freshness and flavor instead of unnecessary shortcuts.

The people behind the bread

Our bakers start the day before sunrise, preparing dough and getting the ovens ready. Our front-of-house team helps neighbors find something to enjoy and answers questions about pre-orders.
